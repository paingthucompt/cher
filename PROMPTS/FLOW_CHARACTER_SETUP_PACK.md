# Google Flow Setup - Character Prompt Pack

## Purpose

Flow ထဲမှာ video မထုတ်ခင် Characters ကိုအရင်တည်ဆောက်ရန်။ Character တစ်ယောက်စီကို သီးခြား create/reference လုပ်ပြီးမှ Scene prompt ထဲခေါ်သုံးရမည်။

## Global Character Rules

- Ultra-realistic cinematic historical Myanmar style.
- Consistent face, body, costume, and emotional behavior across all episodes.
- No fantasy, no magic, no modern clothing, no sci-fi devices.
- Dialogue, if any, must be natural spoken Burmese only.
- Do not make any character advertise SocialAutoUpload.com.

## CHR001 - Min Thant

**Flow Character Prompt**

```text
Create a consistent character reference for CHR001 Min Thant.

Male Burmese, age 28, slim athletic build, medium skin tone, oval face, calm intelligent dark brown eyes, short clean black hair. He is a royal communication officer in fictional သီရိမာလာနိုင်ငံ. Costume: white traditional Burmese officer tunic, brown longyi, official royal seal belt, leather scroll bag across the body. Posture disciplined, quiet confidence, observant, not flashy.

Visual style: ultra-realistic cinematic historical Myanmar royal mystery, warm natural light, Konbaung-inspired but original fictional design, no fantasy, no magic, no modern objects.

Personality in expression: patient, responsible, logical, honest, slightly worried under control. He speaks little; his face and eyes carry the thought.

Natural Burmese voice examples:
"သက်သေ ရှိလား။"
"အချိန် မရှိဘူး။"
"စာပဲပျောက်တာမဟုတ်ဘူး။ မှတ်တမ်းပါ ပျောက်နေတယ်။"
```

## CHR002 - King Kyawzwa

```text

Male Burmese, age 48, tall, strong build, square face, black hair with subtle gray, calm sharp eyes, clean mature face with no visible scars or wounds. Traditional Burmese royal king inspired by 16th-century Toungoo / Hanthawaddy Burmese warrior-king visual language: wrapped Burmese gaung baung style headcloth, ornate Burmese martial regalia, red-and-gold paso/longyi lower garment, dignified posture, minimal facial expressions, quiet authority. Do not use Thai chada crowns, Thai dancer costumes, Chinese imperial crowns, dragon motifs, or East Asian emperor robes.
```
