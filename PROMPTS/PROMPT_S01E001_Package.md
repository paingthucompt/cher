# Prompt Package - S01E001

## Episode

S01E001 - ပျောက်ဆုံးသော အမိန့်

## Product Feature

PF001 - Multi-platform publishing

## Story Metaphor

တော်ဝင်အမိန့်တစ်စောင်ကို route များစွာသို့တစ်ပြိုင်နက်ပို့ရမည့် relay system ကိုပြသည်။

## Google Flow / Veo Prompt Skeleton

Create a 30-90 second Myanmar royal mystery short-form scene. Character dialogue must be natural spoken Burmese only, with no English dialogue and no bookish Burmese. Show a royal messenger horse returning without the messenger or order. Use warm palace morning light, tense silence, and close-up details of the missing bell and sealed envelope. End with archive shelf silhouette and a clean cut to black.

Do not ask Veo/Flow to generate, redraw, imitate, or display the SocialAutoUpload logo, opening title, or end card inside the story video. Add the official opening/title and BRAND001 end card separately in post-production. The official logo asset name is `logo.png`.


## မြန်မာ Dialogue Direction

- Dialogue must be in natural spoken Burmese only.
- Keep lines short and realistic.
- Use role-appropriate respect words.
- Do not let any character explain SocialAutoUpload.com directly.

Example:

Min Thant:

"စာက ပျောက်တာမဟုတ်ဘူး။ မှတ်တမ်းထဲကနေ ဖျောက်ထားတာ။"
