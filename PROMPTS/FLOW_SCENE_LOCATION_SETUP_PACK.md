- Fictional သီရိမာလာနိုင်ငံ, Myanmar-culture-inspired royal world.

- No magic, no dragons, no fantasy powers, no modern city, no sci-fi devices.
- Warm gold, teak brown, ivory white, deep red are main world colors.
