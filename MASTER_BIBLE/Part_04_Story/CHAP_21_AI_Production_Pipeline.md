## 21.10 Quality Assurance (QA)

QA Team က အောက်ပါ (10) ချက် စစ်ရမယ်။

- Character Face Consistency
- Costume
- Location
- Lighting
- Dialogue
- Timeline
- Canon
- Continuity
- Render Quality
- Audio Sync

QA Score

| Score | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 90+ | Release |
| 80-89 | Revision |
| Below 80 | Rebuild |
