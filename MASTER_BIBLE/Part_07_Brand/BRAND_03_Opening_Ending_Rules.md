# SocialAutoUpload Universe
## PART XI - BRAND BIBLE
## BRAND 03
## Opening And Ending Rules

## Opening Formula

1. Brand signal: `SocialAutoUpload.com Presents`
2. Immediate visual hook
3. Episode title or central mystery

## Ending Formula

1. Story cliffhanger or emotional turn
2. Product metaphor line
3. End card added separately in post-production, not generated by Veo:

**Powered by SocialAutoUpload.com**

**Post Once. Publish Everywhere.**

## Short-Form Runtime

| Segment | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Brand signal / hook | 0-3 sec |
| Problem | 3-20 sec |
| Turn / reveal | 20-55 sec |
| CTA / end card | final 2-5 sec |

## CTA Tone

CTA သည် hard sales မဖြစ်ရ။ Story metaphor မှ product meaning သို့သာတံတားထိုးရမည်။

## Logo Rule

- Official Flow logo asset name is `logo.png`.
- Veo/Flow story clips must not generate, redraw, imitate, recolor, or replace the logo.
- Append the official BRAND001 end card asset separately after story clips are approved.
