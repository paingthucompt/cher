## Marketing Strategy

## Goal

မြန်မာယဉ်ကျေးမှုအခြေခံ AI ဇာတ်လမ်းတိုများမှတဆင့် SocialAutoUpload.com ၏ core value ကို viewer မှတ်မိစေရန်။

## Audience

- Content creators
- Small businesses
- Agencies
- E-commerce sellers
- Multi-platform page/channel managers

## Content Strategy

- Episodes must be standalone.
- Every episode must show a communication/publishing problem.
- Every episode must imply a SocialAutoUpload.com feature through RCD metaphor.
- Every episode must end with a clear brand signal.

## Platform Strategy

- Facebook Reels: broad Myanmar audience discovery
- TikTok: fast hook / recurring mystery moments
- YouTube Shorts: season playlist and search discovery
- Instagram Reels: visual identity and creator audience
- Telegram: community distribution and direct bot/product relevance
